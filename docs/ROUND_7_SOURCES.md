Source pages can be revised independently. Benefit descriptions are concise original summaries of survival benefits, not complete policy terms. Percentages refer to the policy's sum insured as specified by each source. They are not returns on premium, IRRs, bank deposit rates, or directly comparable net returns. Alternative maturity bases and previously paid benefits matter. The app does not multiply these summaries by a member's desired sum insured to fabricate a personal projection.

All personal premium fields remain `null`. Illustrative premium tables from brochures are deliberately not reused as personal quotes. Tax eligibility/limits are not calculated in this catalog. Acceptance, official insurance age, exclusions, debt deductions, death benefits and surrender terms require the insurer's full documentation.
